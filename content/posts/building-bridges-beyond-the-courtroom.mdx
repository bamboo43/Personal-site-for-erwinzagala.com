Lawyers solve access problems with advice and advocacy. Builders solve them with products and pathways. I've found both belong in the same toolkit.

## The same problem, different doors

People stall for the same reasons across domains: they don't know the next step, they don't trust the process, or the system wasn't designed for them. In law, that might mean a confusing contract. In health, it might mean finding the right clinic.

**Project Gateway** and related work like **Patient Bridge** and **Dental Atlas** are attempts to lower those doors — connecting patients, providers, and communities with less friction.

## What practice teaches product

Sitting with clients teaches patience with ambiguity. Shipping a product teaches ruthlessness about scope. Together, they keep ventures human: useful first, clever second.
